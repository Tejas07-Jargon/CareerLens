4. Claim vs Evidence Comparison & Contradiction Detection

  • Under-Supported Claims (Neutral Flag): Agar candidate resume me 'Expert' claim kare lekin code evidence minimal ho (e.g. AWS 18%), system bina accusation ke neutral recommendation deta hai: "Claim appears under-supported. Recommendation: Add a demonstrable AWS project or reduce proficiency claim."
  • Unlisted / Stronger Skills: Agar candidate ne 'Beginner' likha hai ya resume me nahi likha lekin repos me 5+ active projects hain, system suggest karta hai: "Evidence suggests stronger proficiency than stated."
  • Fair Missing Data Handling: 'No evidence found', 'Evidence unavailable' (GitHub not connected) aur 'Contradictory evidence' ko alag-alag treat kiya jata hai.
